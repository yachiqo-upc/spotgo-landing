Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SpotGo handles personal information when you use the SpotGo Driver and SpotGo Parking Admin mobile apps and related services. It also describes the language preference stored by this landing page.

Information We Collect

Depending on your role and how you use SpotGo, information may include details you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, account information, vehicle details, reservations, payment method or transaction information, and information submitted while managing parking operations. We may also collect device and service usage information, including parking activity.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide and maintain SpotGo, support accounts, parking searches, reservations and payments, display operational information, send service and security notices, protect users, and improve the apps and related services.

Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers who help operate SpotGo, when disclosure is required by law, when needed to protect rights or safety, or as you direct.

Data Security

We use safeguards intended to protect information. No method of transmitting or storing information can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide SpotGo and meet legal, accounting, security, dispute resolution, and operational requirements. You may contact us to request deletion. Some information may be retained where the law permits or requires it.

Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Contact us at privacy@spotgo.com to make a request.

Cookies and Local Storage

This landing page stores your selected language (en_US or es_419) in your browser's local storage on this device so the page can reopen in that language. The mobile apps and related services may store information needed for sessions, account security, and preferences.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised version here and update the date above when it changes.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Contact us at privacy@spotgo.com.
