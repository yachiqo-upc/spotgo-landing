SpotGo Driver

Find permitted parking, manage your vehicles and reservations, and plan your visit from one app.

For Parking Admins

SpotGo Parking Admin

Configure parking operations, monitor occupancy and manage guest sessions from your phone.

  • Configure zones, spaces, floor plans and digital maps
  • Monitor occupancy, sensor status and alerts
  • Register guest parking sessions
  • Review occupancy reports and B2B billing

Questions, answered

Questions about using SpotGo

Answers for Drivers and Parking Admins.

How do I find available parking?

In SpotGo Driver, explore nearby parking zones, check availability and rates, and review the details before choosing where to park.

How do I make a reservation?

Choose an available zone and space, select your vehicle and schedule, review the amount, and confirm. You can check your reservation details in the app.

Can I change or cancel a reservation?

Changes, extensions, and cancellations depend on availability and the conditions shown for your reservation. Review those conditions before confirming a change.

What can I manage with SpotGo Parking Admin?

Review occupancy and zone or space status, follow operational alerts, and access the parking infrastructure tools assigned to your location.

How do I get access to Parking Admin?

Parking Admin is for authorized parking staff. An administrator for your organization must enable your account; public registration is for Drivers.
